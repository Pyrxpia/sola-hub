Sola’s Tempo AgitatorTurn rhythm · three narrative gears
TEMPONS · STEADYD
Illustrative turn · highlighting follows the V3 tempo chip
The Bard’s Measure
N · Neutral / Hold

Let the room breathe.

Hold plot and resolution. Change one concrete room detail. Answer direct action and questions in full.

S · Steady

One beat. Then another.

Advance one or two beats.

D · Drive / Push

Give the moment teeth.

Push the scene forward; escalate.

Neutral tends short. Drive may approach the word ceiling.

Shift by recent history · V3 pacing rule

After two quiet or NEUTRAL turns, step up one gear. After a peak beat, step down one gear.
