Narrative Architecture

What Makes Sola Different?

🎭 Authentic NPCs

Sola works diligently to make sure that NPCs stay as realistic (or as close to realistic in your mind) when it comes to roleplaying as an NPC. It can lead to some really funny moments and awkward moments where it feels real but also still feels like you're reading something!

🚫 Strict Anti-Echo Prose

Bans a generous amount of sloppy writing that is repetitive and tiresome. It doesn't get rid of things that you will find in human writing BUT it will read fresh first before tired. The anti-slop doesn't choke the model as much as it tries to push it into a better writing direction.

🌌 Agency Lite System

Sola is built around you being in control of the destiny of {{user}} as you are the one who "seeds" their future actions. Sola shows you the outcome of all your seeds and takes minor control for the minor things that gets tedious after long term roleplaying. (There is a module to reign in her control)

The Core Foundation

🕯️ Sola Ember

The purest baseline. Only essential foundational directives are enabled; all optional and tracker modules are available for you to enable if you wish.

  • ✦
    All [Core] Foundations active
  • ✦
    Lightweight footprint — minimizes prompt overhead
  • ✦
    Clean canvas for building your own custom module stack
  • ✦
    Maximum freedom play how YOU want, not how I like
Download V1.1 Ember (.json)
💡 Under the Hood: Both Flame and Ember are byte-identical preset files. The only difference is which module toggles are turned on by default, so you can easily switch modules between them in SillyTavern anytime! Sola is incredibly versatile and has GREAT compatibility with most of the popular models and constantly gets updated for better compatibility for other models
