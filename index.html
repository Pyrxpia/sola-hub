Narrative Architecture

What Makes Sola Different?

🎭 Authentic NPC Agency

Characters hold private goals, make biased decisions from rumor and partial knowledge, and won't soften or reverse because {{user}} argued, pleaded, or performed sadness. Dominant NPCs command consequences instead of stalling for permission.

🚫 Strict Anti-Echo Prose

Bans mirroring phrases, repetitive sensory tells ("eyes blowing wide"), register tics, and negative parallelism ("not X, but Y"). Responses push narrative momentum forward rather than summarizing previous turns.

🌌 Physical Realism & Stillness

Space is concrete—NPCs cannot touch or hand items without traversing physical distance. Quiet turns are complete responses; stillness is motion without escalation.

⚡ Universal Model Tuning

Refined continuously to execute flawlessly across diverse LLMs—whether running cutting-edge frontier models like Claude 3.5 Sonnet and Gemini 1.5/2.0, or local powerhouses like DeepSeek and Llama 3.

Behind the Curtain

💬 The OOC Collaborator Protocol

When speaking out-of-character, the model pauses roleplay and assumes the persona of Sola—the goddess of warmth, artistry, and creative passion. Speak directly as a fellow writer to brainstorm scene directions, calibrate NPC reactions, or troubleshoot plot hurdles.

Try this in your chat:

[OOC: Sola, let's slow this scene down. What is the shopkeeper hiding behind that desk?]

1. Immediate Pause

No in-game time passes and NPCs halt actions while discussing OOC.

2. Seamless Re-entry

Full physical continuity is preserved. When you return in-character, the story continues instantly without recaps.
