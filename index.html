Narrative Architecture

What Makes Sola Different?

🎭 Authentic NPCs

Sola works diligently to make sure that NPCs stay as realistic (or as close to realistic in your mind) when it comes to roleplaying as an NPC. It can lead to some really funny moments and awkward moments where it feels real but also still feels like you're reading something!

🚫 Strict Anti-Echo Prose

Bans a generous amount of sloppy writing that is repetitive and tiresome. It doesn't get rid of things that you will find in human writing BUT it will read fresh first before tired. The anti-slop doesn't choke the model as much as it tries to push it into a better writing direction.

🌌 Agency Lite System

Sola is built around you being in control of the destiny of {{user}} as you are the one who "seeds" their future actions. Sola shows you the outcome of all your seeds and takes minor control for the minor things that get tedious after long-term roleplaying. (There is a module to reign in her control)

Version History

📜 Changelog

🔥 Version 1.2 (Flame & Ember) Current Release

A major architectural leap forward, introducing dynamic recency synchronization, unified showrunner telemetry, full anti-therapy behavioral gates, and streamlined POV token efficiency:

🧠 Dynamic Recency & Macro Engine

  • 21-Variable Dynamic Synchronization: Implemented a full {{setvar}} / {{getvar}} macro pipeline linking active telemetry trackers, POV modes, tone colors, and HUD elements with zero configuration drift.
  • Context-Aware Recency Stack: Tracker Reminder 📌 and Story Review ⭐ now dynamically mirror your exact enabled modules, eliminating token waste from inactive prompts.

🎛️ Showrunner Master HUD & Multi-NPC Telemetry

  • Unified Showrunner Master HUD: Consolidates all active state trackers into an obsidian & warm amber console wrapped in a single <sola_hud> block with clean, glanceable telemetry badges (T# F# D# Y#).
  • Native Multi-NPC Telemetry: Single-container tracking for multiple active characters within <fengine> and <bond>, preventing tag bloat and parse breakage.
  • Showrunner {{char}} Matrix & Self-Audit (<char_matrix>): Deep diagnostic tracker auditing persona adherence, subtext, and active psychological masks.

🛡️ Anti-Therapy & Grounded Dialogue Overhaul

  • Comprehensive Anti-Therapy Rules: Banned clinical counseling habits including reflective listening ("What I hear you saying..."), standalone validation beats ("That's completely valid"), permission-granting ("You don't have to answer that"), and counselor interrogation.
  • Human Ineptitude in Heavy Moments: NPCs have explicit permission to be awkwardly human—getting uncomfortable, saying the wrong thing, offering a drink or silence, or changing the subject rather than turning into a supportive therapist.
  • Let the Beat Sit: NPCs deliver sharp observations in one concise line, strictly preventing paragraph-long psychological lectures or unsolicited emotional debriefs.

👁️ Streamlined POV Architecture (20%–50% Token Savings)

  • Dense Declarative Blueprints: Replaced redundant metadata blocks and tautological rules across all 5 POVs (1st Person, 2nd Person, Freaky Hybrid, 3rd Person Past, 3rd Person Present) with dense, high-impact constraints.
  • Third-Person Present Optimized: Slashed token overhead nearly in half (157 words → 81 words) while maintaining hardened anti-past-drift grammar defenses.

⚡ Reasoning & Thinking Optimization

  • Lightning Story Review ⚡: New lightweight thinking module designed for fast reasoning models, enforcing one-pass telegraphic planning without full-prose drafting or token burn.
  • Eastern Model Story Grounding: Added [Module] Flash Story Grounding tailored specifically for GLM, MiniMax, and DeepSeek, alongside the Stop Thinking So Hard 🧠 countermeasure.

⚙️ Sola V1.2 Regex Companion Pack

  • Showdown Markdown Isolation: Wrapped tracker cards in isolated <div> containers, resolving multi-card HTML breakage and ensuring flawless rendering on desktop and mobile SillyTavern.
  • New Visual Cards: Full support for <char_matrix> trait audits and multi-NPC feeling engine chips.
