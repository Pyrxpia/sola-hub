Narrative Architecture

What Makes Sola Different?

🎭 Authentic NPCs

Sola works diligently to make sure that NPCs stay as realistic (or as close to realistic in your mind) when it comes to roleplaying as an NPC. It can lead to some really funny moments and awkward moments where it feels real but also still feels like you're reading something!

🚫 Strict Anti-Echo Prose

Bans a generous amount of sloppy writing that is repetitive and tiresome. It doesn't get rid of things that you will find in human writing BUT it will read fresh first before tired. The anti-slop doesn't choke the model as much as it tries to push it into a better writing direction.

🌌 Agency Lite System

Sola is built around you being in control of the destiny of {{user}} as you are the one who "seeds" their future actions. Sola shows you the outcome of all your seeds and takes minor control for the minor things that get tedious after long-term roleplaying. (There is a module to reign in her control)

Behind The Curtain

🎭 OOC Story-Boarding Protocol

✧ Step Behind the Curtain With Your Co-Author

Sola is designed from the ground up as an enthusiastic, deeply invested Co-Author and creative director—not just a sterile text generator. When you're mid-story and hit a crossroads, you don't have to guess or struggle with awkward trial-and-error swipes. You can step behind the curtain anytime.

Using ((OOC: ...)) signals Sola to immediately pause in-character roleplay. In-world time freezes, HUD state trackers are suspended, and Sola speaks to you directly as a passionate narrative craft nerd—eager to brainstorm plot twists, calibrate character friction, pitch scene alternatives, or unpack complex NPC motives before you commit.

💡 Why Story-Board Out-of-Character?
  • Zero Story Pollution: Brainstorm wild plot branches without cluttering the fiction with discarded dialogue or false starts.
  • Psychological Diagnostics: Ask Sola what an NPC is hiding, why they hesitated, or what boundary they're protecting under their mask.
  • Director Pacing Adjustments: Tell Sola to slow down, build unhurried atmospheric dread, or turn up the dialogue heat before writing the next beat.
  • Flawless Re-Entry: When you're ready, simply post your next in-character line or action. Sola picks up seamlessly with zero recaps or memory amnesia.

📋 Ready-to-Use Story-Boarding Prompts

Click "Copy Prompt" and paste directly into your chat whenever you want to brainstorm or direct the scene:

Scenario Branching & Brainstorming
((OOC: Sola, let's pause here. What are 2-3 compelling ways this confrontation could develop based on what Kami is hiding?))
Pacing & Atmospheric Calibration
((OOC: Let's slow the pacing way down for the next turn. Let the silence breathe and focus heavily on physical hesitation before anyone speaks.))
Mixed Input (Director Note + In-Character Action)
((OOC: Let's raise the emotional friction here.)) I step into the doorway, refusing to look away.

🔥 Version 2.0 (Flame & Ember) Current Release

A major architectural evolution introducing Embodied Reality, a balanced 16-option Trajectory Fork, modular Action-Anchored Dialogue, dual Story Grounding, streamlined reasoning tiers, and canonical 16-color visual harmony:

🌍 Embodied Reality & Physical Grounding

  • Embodied Reality Core Directive: Unified physical space traversal and NPC knowledge limits into one cohesive directive. NPCs perceive strictly what is physically available to them and must traverse concrete space to whisper, touch, or hand items to {{user}}.
  • Anti-Echo & Single-Pass Repetition Ban: Folded the 6-gesture sensory repetition ban directly into Anti-Echo, forbidding semantic re-skins and premise echo.
  • Post-History Pacing & Stillness: Anchored stillness directives directly at the generation threshold: quiet turns are complete responses; stillness is motion without escalation.

🎲 Trajectory Fork (Balanced 16-Option Engine)

  • Zero-Collision Architecture: Completely redesigned the trajectory engine into 16 discrete, inlined permutations. Eliminates duplicate rolls (never Main A + Sub A) and physically erases unrolled routes from the LLM prompt.
  • Solo & Compound Trajectories: 25% chance of a focused single route (pure execution without sub-current) and 75% chance of compound routes with layered subtext or physical beats.
  • Active Directorial Commitment: Enforces strict scene momentum lock in Story Review, preventing models from kitchen-sinking unassigned narrative vectors.

🗣️ Voice, Idiolect & Bare Speech Modularization

  • Rhythmic Axis Protocol: Upgraded the 3-Axis Idiolect Engine (Diction, Rhythmic, Evasion) to emphasize thought assembly, spoken friction, phonetic grain, and cadence differentiation.
  • Action-Anchored Dialogue Module: Extracted speech tag and vocal texture label bans into a dedicated toggleable module ([Module] Voice: Action-Anchored Dialogue (Zero Tags) 🏷️) wired via {{mod_notags}}.
  • Pre & Post-History Story Grounding: Twin modular toggles enforcing Anti-Bathos and human social friction ("Humanism > Style") without conversational runoff.

⚡ Reasoning Tiers & Thinking Countermeasures

  • Stop Thinking So Hard 🧠 (Hard Limits): Strict ~150-word, 5-bullet telegraphic budget banning trial drafting, scene rehearsal, hedging, and moralizing.
  • Lightning Story Review ⚡: Forward-only directorial speed-run guaranteeing physical grounding, {{char}} boundary fidelity, and 3-Anchor locks (OPEN, CRUX, CLOSE).

🎨 Canonical 16-Color UI & V2 Regex Pack

  • 16-Color Canonical Alignment: Unified all palette chips, HUD badges, and scene ephemera across Day (Golden-Hour) and Night (Obsidian) palettes.
  • V2 Sola Character Artwork: High-resolution official character portrait released in full fidelity.

Version 1.2 (Flame & Ember) Archive

A major architectural leap forward, introducing dynamic recency synchronization, unified showrunner telemetry, full anti-therapy behavioral gates, and streamlined POV token efficiency:

🧠 Dynamic Recency & Macro Engine

  • 21-Variable Dynamic Synchronization: Implemented a full {{setvar}} / {{getvar}} macro pipeline linking active telemetry trackers, POV modes, tone colors, and HUD elements with zero configuration drift.
  • Context-Aware Recency Stack: Tracker Reminder 📌 and Story Review ⭐ now dynamically mirror your exact enabled modules, eliminating token waste from inactive prompts.

🎛️ Showrunner Master HUD & Multi-NPC Telemetry

  • Unified Showrunner Master HUD: Consolidates all active state trackers into an obsidian & warm amber console wrapped in a single <sola_hud> block with clean, glanceable telemetry badges (T# F# D# Y#).
  • Native Multi-NPC Telemetry: Single-container tracking for multiple active characters within <fengine> and <bond>, preventing tag bloat and parse breakage.
  • Showrunner {{char}} Matrix & Self-Audit (<char_matrix>): Deep diagnostic tracker auditing persona adherence, subtext, and active psychological masks.

🛡️ Anti-Therapy & Grounded Dialogue Overhaul

  • Comprehensive Anti-Therapy Rules: Banned clinical counseling habits including reflective listening ("What I hear you saying..."), standalone validation beats ("That's completely valid"), permission-granting ("You don't have to answer that"), and counselor interrogation.
  • Human Ineptitude in Heavy Moments: NPCs have explicit permission to be awkwardly human—getting uncomfortable, saying the wrong thing, offering a drink or silence, or changing the subject rather than turning into a supportive therapist.
  • Let the Beat Sit: NPCs deliver sharp observations in one concise line, strictly preventing paragraph-long psychological lectures or unsolicited emotional debriefs.

👁️ Streamlined POV Architecture (20%–50% Token Savings)

  • Dense Declarative Blueprints: Replaced redundant metadata blocks and tautological rules across all 5 POVs (1st Person, 2nd Person, Freaky Hybrid, 3rd Person Past, 3rd Person Present) with dense, high-impact constraints.
  • Third-Person Present Optimized: Slashed token overhead nearly in half (157 words → 81 words) while maintaining hardened anti-past-drift grammar defenses.

⚡ Reasoning & Thinking Optimization

  • Lightning Story Review ⚡: New lightweight thinking module designed for fast reasoning models, enforcing one-pass telegraphic planning without full-prose drafting or token burn.
  • Eastern Model Story Grounding: Added [Module] Flash Story Grounding tailored specifically for GLM, MiniMax, and DeepSeek, alongside the Stop Thinking So Hard 🧠 countermeasure.

⚙️ Sola V1.2 Regex Companion Pack

  • Showdown Markdown Isolation: Wrapped tracker cards in isolated <div> containers, resolving multi-card HTML breakage and ensuring flawless rendering on desktop and mobile SillyTavern.
  • New Visual Cards: Full support for <char_matrix> trait audits and multi-NPC feeling engine chips.
