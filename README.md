# ✧ Sola ✧

**A storyteller and co-author for the stories you want to live in.**

Sola helps you build scenes with characters who have their own wants, voices, and boundaries. You bring the seeds; she brings the world, the people, and the details that help them grow.

[Visit the Sola hub](https://Pyrxpia.github.io/sola-hub/) · [Downloads](#downloads) · [Getting started](#getting-started)

## Find your spark

### 🔥 Flame V4

The full Sola experience: a modular preset with adjustable point of view, agency, prose, worldbuilding, presentation, trackers, and story review. Choose a standard preset and add a companion regex pack, or choose **Complete** to get the Full pack attached.

Flame has five selectable minds: **Riot, Gremlin, Velvet, Mystic, and Scholar**. Leave them all off for **Golden**, Sola’s default voice. The current release has Riot enabled on import; turn Riot off if you want to start with Golden.

### 🧡 Kindling V2

A smaller, scene-focused Sola with one special voice: **Golden**. Kindling includes its own regex scripts and offers optional Feeling Engine and Sola’s Easel prompts.

## Getting started

1. Pick **Flame V4** or **Kindling V2**, then download the file for your app.
2. Import the preset in your app’s preset settings and select it for your chat. In SillyTavern, use **AI Response Configuration → Import**.
3. If you chose standard Flame and want the companion formatting, import the matching Full or Lite regex pack. In SillyTavern, use **Extensions → Regex Extension → Import**.
4. In Flame, choose a mind or leave them all off for Golden. In each **PICK 1** group, choose one option or leave the group off. Turn optional prompts and trackers on as your story needs them.
5. In Kindling, meet Golden and choose any optional prompts you want to use.

Sola’s default **Agency-Lite** approach leaves your character’s major decisions to you while she handles the world, NPCs, and the immediate outcomes of your actions. Flame also offers other agency options if you want a different balance.

## Behind the curtain

Flame’s optional **Sola Directors OOC** prompt lets you pause the story and talk through a scene, character motivation, or possible next beat with Sola. Enable it and try:

```text
((OOC: Sola, let's pause here. What are a couple of directions this scene could take?))
```

Kindling does not have that dedicated module, but you can still ask Golden for direction. Flame also has optional trackers, the Easel, and review tools for following the threads of a story.
