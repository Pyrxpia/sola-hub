# ✧ Sola ✧

**A storyteller and co-author for the stories you want to live in.**

Sola helps you build scenes with characters who have their own wants, voices, and boundaries. You bring the seeds; she brings the world, the people, and the details that help them grow.

[Visit the Sola hub](https://Pyrxpia.github.io/sola-hub/) · [Downloads](#downloads) · [Getting started](#getting-started)

## Find your spark

### 🔥 Flame V5

The full Sola experience: a modular preset with adjustable point of view, agency, prose, worldbuilding, presentation, trackers, Sola’s Board, the Easel, and story review. Choose a standard preset and add a companion regex pack, or choose **Complete** to get a pack attached (Full or Lite).

Flame V5 has eight minds: **Riot, Golden, Gremlin, Velvet, Mystic, Scholar, Kitsune, and Doomer**. Riot is picked when you import. In Lumiverse, choose another in the **Sola’s Mind** pick; in SillyTavern and Tavo, turn on one mind prompt.

### 🧡 Kindling V3

A smaller, scene-focused Sola with one special voice: **Golden**. Kindling V3 is brought up to date with Flame V5’s writing, includes its own regex scripts, and offers optional Feeling Engine and Sola’s Easel prompts.

## Getting started

1. Pick **Flame V5** or **Kindling V3**, then download the file for your app.
2. Import the preset in your app’s preset settings and select it for your chat. In SillyTavern, use **AI Response Configuration → Import**.
3. If you chose standard Flame and want the companion formatting, import the matching Full or Lite regex pack. In SillyTavern, use **Extensions → Regex Extension → Import**.
4. In Flame, choose a mind and set the options you want: in Lumiverse with the preset’s switches and picks (each has a short description), in SillyTavern and Tavo by turning prompts on or off. Turn trackers and optional modules on as your story needs them.
5. In Kindling, meet Golden and choose any optional prompts you want to use.

Sola’s default **Agency-Lite** approach leaves your character’s major decisions to you while she handles the world, NPCs, and the immediate outcomes of your actions. Flame also offers other agency options if you want a different balance. A few Flame modules need Lumiverse itself (Council Bridge, Memory, Now Playing, Pocket, and Crossroads), so the SillyTavern and Tavo files leave them out.

## Behind the curtain

Flame’s optional **Sola Directors OOC** module (off by default) lets you pause the story and talk through a scene, character motivation, or possible next beat with Sola. Switch it on and try:

```text
((OOC: Sola, let's pause here. What are a couple of directions this scene could take?))
```

Sola also answers to `[[ ... ]]`, `OOC:`, and `/ooc`. For something lighter, **Sola Chatterbox** (on by default) adds a little text thread under each reply; write `(U: ...)` in your message to text her back.

Kindling does not have the dedicated OOC module, but you can still ask Golden for direction. Flame also has trackers, Sola’s Board, the Easel, and review tools for following the threads of a story.
