## 🔥 Flame vs. 🕯️ Ember

- **🔥 Sola Flame (Author Edition):** The exact way I like to play with Sola. Feature-complete with narrative modules, showrunner trackers, and story review active out of the box.
- **🕯️ Sola Ember (Core Foundation):** Pristine baseline with only essential `[Core]` foundation directives active. All optional and tracker modules disabled.
- *Note: Both presets are byte-identical under the hood; they differ only in which module toggles are active by default.*
